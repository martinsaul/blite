Screen sharing built for friends

Everything you need to watch together, and nothing between you and your friends.

  • Up to 4K at 60 fps

    One tap picks 720p, 1080p or 4K at 30 fps for sharp text or 60 fps for smooth games, or add your own preset. Hardware encoding on NVIDIA, Intel and AMD.

  • Share what you want

    Share your entire screen or one window, picked from a list with app icons. A window shares its app's sound too, automatically. All screens combined is there when you need it.

  • Everyone can share at once

    Everyone in a room can go live at the same time. Watch them all in one window, pin the one that matters, or pop someone out into their own window.

  • Adapts to each viewer

    Viewers whose connection or computer can't take the top size get a smaller one. Set each person's video quality and volume yourself.

  • Discord built in

    Connect Discord to go live or watch in your voice channel with one click, without pasting links.

  • Free, no account

    Start a session and send the link. No sign-up, no ads, no trackers. The app speaks 28 languages and picks yours automatically.

How it works

No streaming service in the middle. The server only introduces people to each other; your video goes straight to your friends.

Peer to peer

Your screen goes straight to the people in your session, not through a streaming service. Our server only introduces people to each other.

Privacy and security →

Relays for bigger rooms

In a small room everyone sends their video to everyone. To grow past that, members with a fast upload volunteer as relays that forward everyone's video.

Set up a relay →

End-to-end encrypted

On by default: each sender encrypts its media with keys only room members get. Read the safety code aloud once: if everyone sees the same numbers, nobody is intercepting the connection.

Encryption and safety codes →

Sharing with a bigger group? Set up a relay

Above about 5 people, every sharer's upload gets stretched thin. A relay fixes that: someone with a fast upload volunteers with one switch in the app, or you run a spare PC or a small server as your group's relay. It works on bellion.app, no server of your own needed.

Discord integration

Go live right in your voice channel

Connect Discord once, and Bellion Share follows the voice channel you're in, so your friends there find your stream without anyone pasting a link.

  • One click to go live in your voice channel.
  • One click to watch whoever is sharing there.
  • Bellion Share only reads which voice channel you're in, not your messages, servers or friends.

Android beta

Android 10 or newer · Google Play closed beta, open to anyone

Get it on Google Play (beta)

Bellion Share for Android (beta): watch streams and join rooms from your phone. It is watch-only for now: to share your screen or start a room, use Windows or Linux.

On Google Play it is a closed beta that anyone can join; it will open to everyone later.

  1. Signed in to the same Google account your phone uses, open the Bellion Share testers group and click Join group.
  2. Open the test page and click Become a tester.
  3. Install from the Play Store link on that page, or from Bellion Share on Google Play. The listing can take a few minutes to appear after you opt in.

Google Play keeps the app up to date. Tap a share link, or scan the QR code of a room, to join it.

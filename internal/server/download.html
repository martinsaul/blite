Screen sharing built for friends

Everything you need to watch together, and nothing between you and your friends.

  • Up to 4K at 60 fps

    Pick 720p, 1080p or 4K, tuned for sharp text or smooth games at 60 fps. Hardware encoding on NVIDIA, Intel and AMD.

  • Share what you want

    Go live shares your main monitor. The Share menu also offers all screens combined, one monitor, or a window, which shares that window's audio too.

  • Everyone can share at once

    Everyone in a room can go live at the same time, and you watch them in separate windows or all in one grid.

  • Adapts to each viewer

    Viewers whose connection or computer can't take the top size get a smaller one. Set each person's video quality and volume yourself.

  • Discord built in

    Connect Discord to go live or watch in your voice channel with one click, without pasting links.

  • Free, no account

    Start a session and send the link. No sign-up, no ads, no trackers.

A look inside

One small window: go live, send the link, and see everyone in the call at a glance.

How it works

No streaming service in the middle. The server only introduces people to each other; your video goes straight to your friends.

Peer to peer

Your screen goes straight to the people in your session, not through a streaming service. When a direct path is blocked, a TURN server forwards the encrypted traffic.

Privacy and security →

Relays for bigger rooms

In a small room everyone sends their video to everyone. To grow past that, members with a fast upload volunteer as relays that forward everyone's video.

How relays work →

End-to-end encrypted

On by default: each sender encrypts its media with keys only room members get. Read the safety code aloud once: if everyone sees the same numbers, nobody is intercepting the connection.

Encryption and safety codes →

Discord integration

Go live right in your voice channel

Connect Discord once, and Bellion Share follows the voice channel you're in, so your friends there find your stream without anyone pasting a link.

  • One click to go live in your voice channel.
  • One click to watch whoever is sharing there.
  • Bellion Share only reads which voice channel you're in, not your messages, servers or friends.

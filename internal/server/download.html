Screen sharing built for friends

  • Share a monitor, a window or app audio at up to 4K and 60 fps, with hardware encoding on NVIDIA, Intel and AMD.
  • Peer to peer and end-to-end encrypted: your screen goes straight to your friends, not through a streaming service.
  • Everyone in a room can share at the same time, and watch in separate windows or one grid.
  • Connect Discord to go live or watch in your voice channel with one click.
  • Free, no account needed: start a session and send the link.
  • Open source under the AGPL-3.0: source code on GitHub.

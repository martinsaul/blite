Privacy Policy

Bellion Share is a screen-sharing app for small groups of friends. This policy explains what data it handles. Last updated: November 19, 2026.

What we collect and why

  • Display name and tag. You choose a name; the app adds a random tag (like #1234). Others in your session see it.
  • Session data. To connect people, our server briefly holds who is in which session, along with their network address (IP address and port). Network addresses stay in memory only. So that a server restart doesn't end calls, the server also saves its open sessions to its own disk every 30 seconds: each session's room code, a hash of its password, the voice channel it's tied to (if any), its owner and banned members, and for each member their display name, public identity key, app version and a hash of the token that lets them reconnect. The file only holds sessions that are open: a session leaves it at the next save after it ends (5 minutes after the last person leaves).
  • Your screen and audio. These go directly between the people in a session (peer to peer). When a direct connection isn't possible they pass through our relay server, which forwards them without recording. Sessions are encrypted end to end by default, so the relay can't see them.
  • Discord (optional). If you press "Connect Discord", Discord asks you to let Bellion see which voice channel you are in. We use this only to show a session started in that channel, and to tie your session to it.
  • Discord login. Our server passes the one-time login code to Discord and hands the access token back to the app, without keeping it. The app keeps the token on your computer only. The server keeps the ID of a voice channel tied to a live session until the session ends (in memory, and in the saved sessions described above). We do not read your messages, servers, friends or anything else.
  • Logs and crash reports. The app writes a log on your computer. If the app crashes, it sends the error and the part of that log just before it to our server on the next start. When you press "Send logs" or "Send feedback", it sends the log and your message. Logs contain your display name, your operating system and hardware, your network addresses, and the names and addresses of people you were in a session with. We use them only to fix problems. They are deleted after 30 days. The app removes room passwords from the log before writing it, and from everything it sends. Older versions on Windows wrote the whole share link, including the room password, to the local log when you opened one, so it could reach us in a crash report or when you sent logs.
  • Crash dumps (Windows). If the app crashes, it saves a crash dump on your computer. Dumps are never uploaded. They don't contain the app's full memory, and the app keeps at most two, for up to 14 days. Older versions wrote dumps with the app's full memory, which can include session keys. You can delete them from %LOCALAPPDATA%\Blite\crash.
  • Your IP address and your router. People in a session connect to each other directly, so they learn each other's IP address. To make that easier, the app asks your router to open UDP port 30471 (UPnP, NAT-PMP or PCP) by default; start it with -udp-port 0 to turn that off.
  • Room passwords. The password in a share link is in the part after #, which your browser doesn't send to us. The app sends it to our server over an encrypted connection to check it when you join; the server keeps only a hash of it, in memory and in its saved sessions, until the session ends.
  • Server logs. Our servers log requests, including IP addresses, for security and abuse prevention. These logs are kept only as long as needed for that.
  • Updates. The app checks our update server for new versions. That server sees your IP address and the app version.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use trackers or analytics. We don't record sessions.

Sharing

We share data only with Discord, when you use "Connect Discord", and as required by law.

Your choices

You can disconnect Discord at any time in Discord: User Settings > Authorized Apps > Bellion. What the app keeps on your computer (its settings, the Discord token and logs) is in its settings and log folders, which you can delete. To have your reports deleted earlier, or for any question, contact us at admin@zenithnetwork.com.

Children

Bellion Share is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.

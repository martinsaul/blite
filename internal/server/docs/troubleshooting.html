Troubleshooting and FAQ

Most problems show up as a hint in the People list or a note at the top of the window. Here is what they mean.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

The installer and the app are code-signed (publisher: Martin Saul). Windows SmartScreen trusts a new signature only after enough people have installed it, so for a while it may still warn. Check that the publisher is Martin Saul, then choose More info and Run anyway.

Clicking a share link doesn't open the app

  • Bellion Share has to be installed. The Windows installer and the Linux package register it for share links. If you use the portable zip, start it once so it registers itself, then click the link again.
  • Your browser may ask whether to open Bellion Share; allow it.
  • You can always copy the link, or just its room code, and paste it into Watch someone in the app instead.

Someone shows "can't connect, retrying"

Bellion Share connects people directly. A direct connection can fail when a firewall or a strict router blocks it.

  • A note at the top of the window says whether others can reach you. Bellion Share asks your router to open its UDP port (30471) with UPnP, NAT-PMP or PCP; if your router doesn't allow that, or sits behind another router or your provider's NAT, calls with people behind strict routers may fail.
  • If you run a firewall, allow Bellion Share through it.
  • When a direct path is blocked, a TURN server can forward the traffic (the connection hint then says so). It's still end-to-end encrypted.
  • If it keeps failing, send logs from both sides (see below).

Low quality, stutter or delay

  • The People list rates each connection Good, Fair or Unstable; click someone for the round-trip time and packet loss. Loss usually means an upload or download is full: pick a lower quality preset (Share options), or set someone's video to Low (saves data) in their panel.
  • For games pick a 60 fps preset; for text and code, 30 fps is sharper at the same upload.
  • If your computer can't encode fast enough, Bellion Share lowers the resolution itself and shows a note. Hardware encoding (NVIDIA, Intel, AMD) helps a lot.
  • In a full mesh every sharer uploads once per viewer. Above about 5 people, set up a relay: someone with a fast upload switches on Volunteer as relay at the bottom of the People list, or a spare PC or server relays for your group.
  • "You can't see someone's stream: its quality is too high for your connection or computer right now" means Bellion Share couldn't find a size you can take. It keeps trying.

No sound from someone, or they hear an echo

  • Sharing the entire screen starts without sound: the sharer picks the sound in Share options (or clicks No audio on the home screen). Sharing a window shares that app's sound automatically.
  • "This window's app audio isn't available" means Bellion Share couldn't find the app the window belongs to (for example an app running as administrator). Pick Whole system or Only these apps... instead. With Only these apps..., apps appear in the list while they play audio.
  • Check you're watching them (the eye next to their name), that you haven't muted them (their speaker is struck through) or turned their volume down, and pick the right device under Share options > Play the call on.
  • Whole system leaves out Bellion Share itself, so the call isn't sent back.

Linux: nothing to share, or no windows listed

Bellion Share captures the screen through X11. Wayland sessions aren't supported yet: log in with an X11 (Xorg) session from your login screen's session menu.

I closed the video window

Closing the video window stops watching everyone in it, and Bellion Share asks whether you want to leave the session; choose Cancel to stay. To watch someone again, click the eye next to their name in the People list. Closing a popped-out window puts that person back into the video window.

The safety codes don't match

If someone's safety code differs from yours, the connection may be intercepted. Leave the session and start a new one. Bellion Share also refuses to connect to anyone whose encryption keys don't match their identity, and warns about it.

"The server's certificate isn't from a trusted public authority"

Bellion Share accepts the bellion.app server's certificate only from the public authorities it is issued by (Let's Encrypt, Google Trust Services, SSL.com). This message means something between you and the server shows another certificate so that it can read the connection. Usually that is a company network's proxy or antivirus software that scans HTTPS. Exclude Bellion Share or bellion.app from the antivirus's HTTPS or web scanning, or use another network. On your own server, see certificate pinning.

"Bellion Share ... is required"

Your version is too old to start or join sessions. Press Install and restart in the note (or use Check for updates in the gear menu). When the app only says a newer version is available, you can keep using it and update when convenient; installing during a call restarts the app and rejoins the call.

Sending logs and feedback

In the ? menu at the top right, Send logs to the developers sends your log; when two people can't connect, send logs from both sides. Send feedback... sends an idea or a problem in your own words. Logs contain names and network addresses of the people you were in a session with, but not room passwords; public network addresses are shortened to a code before anything is written or sent. They're used only to fix problems and deleted within 72 hours (see the Privacy Policy).

Android: Google Play says the app isn't available

The Google Play version is a closed beta: Play shows it only to testers. Check that:

  • you joined the Bellion Share testers group with the same Google account your phone uses in the Play Store;
  • you opted in on the test page (Become a tester).

After opting in, the listing can take a few minutes to appear: wait a little, then open Bellion Share on Google Play again. No Google services on your phone? Download the APK instead.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it's free, and no account is needed. Bellion Share is open source under the AGPL-3.0: the source code is on GitHub.

Does it work on macOS or phones?

Not on macOS or iPhone. Bellion Share runs on Windows 10 and 11, and on Linux with X11. On Android 10 or newer, the Android app (beta, on Google Play or as an APK) watches streams and joins rooms; it can't share its screen yet.

Discord integration

Bellion Share can follow the Discord voice channel you're in, so your friends in that channel find your stream without anyone pasting a link.

Connect Discord

  1. In Bellion Share, press Connect Discord.
  2. Your browser opens Discord's authorize page. Approve Bellion there. It only lets Bellion know who you are on Discord.
  3. Join a voice channel in a Discord server that has the Bellion bot. Bellion Share shows that channel within a second or two.

You do this once per computer. Discord doesn't have to run on the same computer: the Bellion bot tells the app which voice channel you're in. To disconnect, open Settings > Disconnect Discord in the app.

Add the Bellion bot to your server

Bellion only sees voice channels in Discord servers that the Bellion bot was added to. If you're in a voice channel and Bellion Share still says Join a Discord voice channel, that server doesn't have the bot yet. Press Copy link for the admin in the app and send it to someone who can manage the server. The link brings them here.

For server admins: adding the bot takes one click, and it asks for only one permission, View Channels.

Add to Discord

  • It sees: who joins and leaves voice channels, and the channel and server names. It keeps the voice channel only for people who connected Bellion Share and have the app running. Everyone else's voice channel changes are dropped at once, without being stored or logged.
  • It can't: read or send messages, join voice channels, hear anything, or change anything in your server. Its only command is /status, which says whether Bellion's servers are up.
  • To remove it: kick it like any member (Server Settings > Members). Bellion then stops seeing that server.

Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Go live in your voice channel

Join a voice channel in Discord. The home screen shows You're in #channel (server) on Discord with two buttons:

  • Share starts a session tied to that channel (or joins the one already there), shares what you picked on the Share your screen card and copies the share link as usual. The password and end-to-end encryption settings from Share options apply too.
  • Join does the same without sharing, so you're in the room and can go live later.

When the channel already has a session, the banner shows how many people are in it.

Watch someone in your voice channel

When someone in your voice channel has gone live, the home screen says who is sharing (for example Sam is sharing their screen), shows who's in the session, and offers a Watch button. If the session has a password, it asks for it.

What Bellion sees

Bellion knows your Discord user ID and name (until you disconnect) and, while the app runs, which voice channel you're in on servers that have the bot. Our server remembers which voice channel a live session is tied to, in memory, until the session ends. Bellion doesn't read your messages, servers or friends. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

To disconnect, open Settings > Disconnect Discord in the app. That deletes the link on our server.

If it doesn't work

  • "Join a Discord voice channel" while you're in one: that server doesn't have the Bellion bot. Ask a server admin to add it; Copy link for the admin in the app gives you the link to send.
  • The browser tab closed before you approved: press Open it again.
  • No Share or Join button: join a voice channel first; text channels don't count.
  • Share links work without Discord: paste one in any chat.
